About Our Business

Dedicated to Excellence with the Caring "Pinay" Touch

Welcome to Pinay Commercial Cleaning And Home Care. We are a passionate, highly professional cleaning team committed to transforming commercial spaces and homes into pristine, hygienic environments that promote well-being and productivity. Proudly serving Richmond, Virginia and Buffalo, New York, we deliver dependable, high-quality cleaning services tailored to the unique needs of homeowners and businesses in our communities.

We pride ourselves on our core values of meticulous attention to detail, complete honesty, absolute dependability, and the use of eco-friendly, family-safe cleaning products. Whether you're looking to maintain a spotless office that leaves a lasting impression or a fresh, comfortable home for your family, our experienced team is here to provide exceptional results with every visit.

To make professional cleaning even more convenient, we now offer flexible service plans that fit your lifestyle and schedule. You can choose from Daily Cleaning, Weekly Cleaning, or Monthly Cleaning services, giving you the freedom to enjoy a consistently clean and healthy environment with the frequency that works best for you.
